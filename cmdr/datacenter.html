Deploy to outage 

      

      
A SONAR alert dispatches the nearest ready cat automatically. The cat drives the ring road, mates its arm to the asset's diagnostic port (never the live bus), runs the checklist and pushes the assessment here, to the site channel and to ADMIN.

    

    

      
Fleet 

      

      
Three kennels on the ring road, each on the generator bus with its own 6 h battery, so cats recharge when the site is dark. Watch on the live map.
